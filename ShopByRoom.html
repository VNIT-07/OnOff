SPATIAL BLUEPRINT: LIVING ROOM

The Conversational Lounge

The modern living environment demands a fluid dialogue between soft ambient wash for evening intimacy and sculptural fixtures that define architectural proportion. High ceilings benefit from monumental sweeping floor arcs and balanced suspensions.

1. Ambient Layer
Diffuse 360° opaline ceiling suspension dimmable from 100% to 10%.
2. Task Layer
Overhead arched floor luminaire positioned behind seating without ceiling anchors.
3. Accent Layer
Solid marble-based tabletop downlight for consoles and low credenzas.
Target Illuminance 150–250 Lux

LIGHTING ARCHITECTURE STANDARDS

Room Illuminance & Kelvin Guide

Every domestic interior fulfills different human requirements. Here are the recommended photometric lux levels, circadian kelvin ranges, and dimming strategies formulated by our Milan lighting engineers.

Environment Illuminance (Lux) Color Temp (Kelvin) Primary Strategy Curated Fixture Types
Living Room 150–250 Lux 2700K (Warm Dim 2200K) Multi-tiered balance of diffuse ceiling bounce and low floor arches. Arco Floor, Forma Pendant, Snoopy Table
Dining Room 200–300 Lux 2400K–2700K Over-table focal illumination without high-angle glare across seated sightlines. Glo-Ball Suspension, Coordinates Lattice, Gustave
Bedroom Suite 100–150 Lux 2200K–2700K Circadian melatonin preservation with tactile low-level bedside sconces. IC Wall Sconce, Bellhop Cordless
Kitchen & Island 300–450 Lux 3000K–3500K (CRI 95+) Culinary task clarity and accurate color rendering across prep surfaces. Coordinates Track, IC Sconce, Linear Pendants
Workspace / Studio 400–500 Lux 3000K–4000K Anti-glare UGR < 16 task luminaires for prolonged creative concentration. Taccia Reflector, Snoopy Table, Gustave
Outdoor & Patio 50–100 Lux 2700K (Dark Sky Compliant) Atmospheric low-level guidance that respects evening starlight and nature. In Vitro Bollard, Captain Flint IP65
